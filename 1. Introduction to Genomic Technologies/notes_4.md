# Genomes: Structure, Function, and Analysis

## Definition and Structure of Genomes
* **Core Definition:** Genomics is the study of genomes—the complete set of molecular material inside cells that defines how bodies function.
* **Human Genome Composition:** Consists of approximately **3 billion** DNA nucleotides (`A`, `C`, `G`, `T`) organized into **23 chromosome pairs**, including the sex chromosomes (`X` and `Y`).

---

## Function and Evolution of Genomes
* **Biological Instructions:** Encodes instructions for organismal development, organ formation, metabolism, and complex functions such as brain construction.
* **Evolutionary Relationships:** Genomes evolve over long timescales; while human genomes are highly similar across individuals, cross-species comparisons reveal evolutionary relationships.

---

## Mapping and Studying Genomes
* **Genome Mapping:** Involves sequencing DNA and identifying genes—inheritable units that encode proteins or functional elements.
* **Modern vs. Traditional Approaches:** Modern genomics analyzes thousands of genes simultaneously, whereas traditional genetics focused on studying one gene at a time.

---

## Technological Advances and Challenges
* **High-Throughput Sequencing:** Advances in sequencing technology enable large-scale experiments that measure thousands of genes concurrently.
* **Big Data Challenges:** Massive data generation creates significant bottlenecks in data analysis, statistical interpretation, and computational infrastructure.
