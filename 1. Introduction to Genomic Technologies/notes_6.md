# Introduction to Genomic Data Science

> **Overview:** Genomic data science is an interdisciplinary field that combines **biology**, **statistics**, and **computer science** to analyze and interpret genomic data.

---

## Definition and Scope of Genomic Data Science
* **Core Focus:** Also known as *computational genomics*, *bioinformatics*, or *statistical genomics*, it centers on the analysis of genomic sequences.
* **End-to-End Workflow:** Encompasses collecting biological samples, sequencing `DNA` or `RNA`, and analyzing the resulting datasets to understand genetic variations and biological functions.

---

## Key Steps in Genomic Data Science
* **Experimental Design:** Crucial for ensuring that collected data can answer specific scientific questions effectively and efficiently.
* **Data Preprocessing & Normalization:** Necessary steps to correct technical biases and errors inherent in sequencing technologies prior to downstream analysis.

---

## Applications and Techniques
* **Sequence Alignment:** Mapping sequence reads to a reference genome to identify genetic variations and quantify gene expression levels.
* **Bioinformatics Software Development:** Building specialized tools and pipelines (e.g., for `RNA-seq` analysis) to process raw genomic data and derive meaningful biological conclusions.
* **Population Genomics:** Investigating genetic differences across populations to uncover drivers of disease susceptibility and phenotypic traits.
* **Integrative Genomics (Systems Biology):** Combining multiple biological data modalities to achieve comprehensive, system-level insights.
